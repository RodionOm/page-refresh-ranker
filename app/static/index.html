How it works

  1. Each row is one page: traffic for days 61–90 and 31–60 before today, plus page details. The last 30 days are never used, so the score can't peek at the answer.
  2. The model scores how likely each page is to lose more than 20% of its impressions.
  3. You get a queue with at most a few pages per client, two plain reasons per page, and flags where a human should look twice.
  4. Mark what you found after review. The run shows how many queued pages really declined.
